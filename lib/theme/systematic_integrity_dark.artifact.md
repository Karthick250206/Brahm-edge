## Brand & Style

The design system is a high-precision, dark-mode framework engineered for enterprise environments where reliability and clarity are paramount. It targets professional users in data-heavy sectors like finance, infrastructure monitoring, and systems engineering.

The aesthetic follows a **Corporate / Modern** approach with a focus on **Tonal Layers**. By utilizing a deep, monochromatic background, the interface minimizes ocular strain during long-term use while allowing the high-integrity primary accents to guide the user's focus. The emotional response is one of stability, authority, and meticulous organization.
